Destination Highlights

See what makes this trip unforgettable

A few of the beautiful stops and landscapes you can look forward to on this Alaska adventure.

Ketchikan

Colorful waterfront charm, rich Native culture, and classic Inside Passage scenery.

Juneau

Home to dramatic mountain views, wildlife excursions, and the famous Mendenhall Glacier.

Skagway

Gold Rush history, scenic rail journeys, and unforgettable mountain landscapes.

Glacier Bay

Scenic cruising among towering glaciers, icy blue waters, and incredible wilderness views.

Denali

Big landscapes, rugged beauty, and the chance to experience Alaska beyond the cruise ports.

Fairbanks

Alaska’s interior hub with riverfront charm and the possibility of late-summer northern lights.
